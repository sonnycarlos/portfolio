My Two-Year Journey at Affiliate World

I was only 24 years old, a fresh graduate, when I joined Affiliate World Conferences. It was my first full-time job, and I never imagined how much it would shape my career and personal life.

Over the past two years, I've grown beyond being a designer. I improved my skills in UI/UX, graphic, motion design, web development, and AI tools. I also had the opportunities to work on-site at Affiliate World Asia in Bangkok, taking on different roles beyond design. That experience was priceless and taught me lessons I couldn't have learned behind a desk.

This journey made me realize that education is very important, but your career isn't defined by your degree. It's built through experience, skills, knowledge, and the willingness to keep learning.

At the age of 24, I earned my first million through my own hard work. It was the biggest achievement of my life and the result of everything I went through to reach that goal. There were distractions, challenges, failures, and lessons along the way. But I learned that if something is meant for you, you'll find a way to get it, no matter what the odds are.

Looking back at the person I was when I started, I never imagined I could achieve something like this. I felt like nobody, both in the eyes of others and in my own eyes. It seemed impossible, but I still believed in myself and took the risk.

If the person I am today could tell my younger self that I would achieve this goal, I wonder if I would even believe it.

The world isn't always fair, and success doesn't always follow the path we expect. Sometimes, you have to take risks, create opportunities, and believe in yourself even when nothing is certain.

I'm grateful for everything I've learned, every challenge I've overcome, and the God who helped me along the way. This journey taught me that sometimes, all you need is faith in yourself, the courage to keep going, and the willingness to learn from every experience.

These two years didn't just make me a better designer.
They changed how I see life, opportunities, and my future.
